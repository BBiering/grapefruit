Questions

Where does the data come from?+

Prices, fundamentals and the symbol universe come from EODHD. Forward-looking catalysts are researched with Perplexity's sonar-pro and stored with their source links, so every claim on a card is traceable.

How often does it refresh?+

The full pipeline runs Monday mornings — universe, fundamentals, bars, winner detection, catalyst scan. Prices refresh nightly, so the dashboard has yesterday's close every morning.

What counts as a past winner?+

A ticker that rose 5× or more in under a week and held the new level. Grapefruit then asks why it happened and whether it was foreseeable beforehand — that second answer is the useful one.

Is the survivorship bias handled?+

No, and we say so on the card. The symbol list is dominated by currently active tickers, so names that ran and then delisted, got acquired or went bankrupt are largely absent. Treat every past winner as filtered through survival.
